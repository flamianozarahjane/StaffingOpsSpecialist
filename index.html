Featured: Paper-to-Digital Forms
Process Improvement & Cost Avoidance

Digitizing 40+ paper forms with tools the company already owned

The production and quality teams ran on paper: inspection checklists, emergency work orders, work orders, and material requests. Approvals were slow, records were hard to trace, and someone had to chase every signature. The original plan was to build this in NetSuite, but it couldn't produce the output the team needed. Instead of buying a new tool, I studied what the company already had and built the full workflow in Adobe Sign and Microsoft Outlook, working fully remote with a U.S. team.

Money saved: no new software to buy and no added subscription fees. I got more out of tools they were already paying for.
Form submitted→Auto-routed to reviewer→Approved or returned→Record stored & traceable
  • Zero new software cost: built on existing Adobe Sign and Outlook licenses
  • 40+ forms converted, with automated review and approval routing
  • Faster approvals with no manual chasing
  • Every record traceable and audit-ready
  • The same approach works for staffing requests, onboarding checklists, and incident reports
